1. Comprehensive Coverage Scope

Every vehicle in the LuxeDrive UAE portfolio is protected by an official Comprehensive Motor Insurance Policy underwritten by Tier-1 UAE Central Bank licensed insurance institutions (Sukoon Insurance / ADNIC).

Coverage automatically incorporates:

  • Own Damage Protection: Complete coverage for accidental damage, impact, mechanical repair, and body restoration resulting from collisions on recognized public highways within the United Arab Emirates.
  • Third-Party Liability: Protection against bodily injury, loss of life, or property damage caused to third parties up to AED 10,000,000 per incident.
  • Perils Coverage: Fire, external explosion, lightning, storm, theft, and malicious damage.
  • Agency Repairs: Strictly authorized official OEM manufacturer workshops (e.g., Al Tayer Motors for Ferrari, Al Habtoor Motors for Bentley, AGMC for Rolls-Royce & BMW).

3. Super Damage Waiver (SDW / Zero-Excess)

Clients seeking complete peace of mind may opt for our proprietary Super Damage Waiver (SDW) at the time of reservation. The SDW package completely waives standard deductibles for bodywork scrapes, minor curb wheel rash, and windshield stone chips, reducing client liability to AED 0 (subject to a valid Dubai Police report).

4. Mandatory Dubai Police Report Requirement

Under the UAE Federal Traffic Law, an official Police Traffic Accident Report is mandatory for any claim to be processed by UAE insurance underwriters:

  • Immediate Notification: In the event of any collision, scrape, or scratch, the Client must immediately halt the vehicle safely and register an accident claim via the Dubai Police Smart App or by calling 999 (Emergency) / 901 (Non-Emergency). In Abu Dhabi, claims must be processed through Saaed (800 72233).
  • Absence of Report: Failure to secure an official police report renders all insurance indemnity null and void. In such circumstances, the Client remains personally liable for the full commercial cost of repairs and vehicle loss of use.

5. VIP Roadside Assistance & Replacement Vehicle

LuxeDrive maintains a dedicated 24/7 flatbed recovery unit and mobile technical response team:

  • Guaranteed roadside arrival within forty-five (45) minutes in central Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
  • Immediate dispatch of an equivalent or superior luxury replacement vehicle within ninety (90) minutes should the vehicle require workshop inspection.
