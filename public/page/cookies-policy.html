1. What Are Cookies and Local Web Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are compact text entries and data structures placed on your device by websites you visit. They are used to retain user preferences, maintain session security, and ensure seamless portal performance.

At LuxeDrive UAE, our digital architecture is purposefully engineered with Zero-Surveillance discipline: we do not permit third-party advertising networks, cross-site trackers, or commercial data brokers to place tracking beacons on your browser.

2. Categories of Storage Utilized

Our platform relies exclusively on two categories of client-side storage:

Cookie / Storage Key Type & Scope Purpose Duration
luxedrive_lang Functional Preference Stores your language choice (English or Arabic with RTL alignment). 12 Months
luxedrive_compare_tray Client Utility Preserves up to 3 selected vehicles in the side-by-side comparison drawer across browser sessions. Session

luxedrive_offline_cache PWA / Offline Service Worker Caches car inventory catalog images and styling so the showroom remains viewable without an internet connection. 30 Days
Zero Commercial Tracking: LuxeDrive does NOT employ Facebook Pixels, Google Remarketing, TikTok pixels, or programmatic behavioral cookies. Your browsing remains strictly confidential.

3. Managing & Disabling Browser Cookies

You retain sovereign authority over cookies. You may configure your browser at any time to reject cookies or purge existing local storage:

  • Apple Safari (iOS / macOS): Settings → Safari → Advanced → Block All Cookies.
  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection (Strict).
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions.

*Please note: Disabling strictly necessary functional storage may reset your language preference back to English and clear your vehicle comparison tray.
