Discretion & Privacy Policy

Our solemn covenant ensuring non-disclosure, encrypted reservation handling, and compliance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021 regarding Personal Data Protection.

🛡️ Standard: UAE PDPL & ISO 27001
🔐 Cipher: AES-256-GCM Zero-Knowledge
🏛️ Compliance Authority: UAE Data Office
📅 Effective: 2026 Edition

1. The LuxeDrive Discretion Covenant

At LuxeDrive UAE, our clientele includes royal dignitaries, heads of state, corporate executives, and high-profile international figures. We recognize that discretion is not an auxiliary feature—it is our foundational standard of operation.

We guarantee that no photographs, videos, transaction amounts, itineraries, or identity disclosures concerning your vehicle acquisition or rental will ever be published, shared with paparazzi, or leveraged for promotional purposes without explicit, notarized written approval.

Non-Disclosure Guarantee: All LuxeDrive employees, chauffeurs, and concierge personnel execute comprehensive, lifelong confidentiality agreements backed by UAE statutory penalties.

2. Information We Collect & Legal Bases

To facilitate vehicle delivery, insurance coverage, and RTA compliance under UAE law, we collect only necessary identifying credentials:

  • Identity Credentials: Full name, VIP title, copy of Passport with UAE entry visa or UAE Emirates ID, and valid driver’s license.
  • Contact Coordinates: Encrypted telephone number, WhatsApp contact, and executive concierge email.
  • Financial & Payment Coordinates: Wire transfer verification details, Central Bank escrow receipts, or credit card pre-authorization tokens (processed strictly via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways).
  • Fleet Telemetry: Vehicle location, speed, and mechanical diagnostics transmitted via private cellular telemetry to ensure vehicle safety, geofence compliance, and emergency assistance dispatch.

3. Client-Side AES-256 Cryptographic Architecture

Unlike conventional automotive rental portals that transmit unencrypted booking forms across cloud servers, LuxeDrive utilizes Client-Side Web Crypto API encryption (AES-256-GCM):

  • Your reservation details are encrypted directly in your browser prior to transmission across public networks.
  • Decryption keys remain isolated within our sovereign, hardware-secured administrative terminals in Dubai.
  • Zero unencrypted personal information is exposed to public analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or external aggregators.

4. GPS Telemetry & Automated Log Scrubbing

In accordance with RTA vehicle security directives and insurance underwriter requirements, our luxury and exotic fleet is equipped with satellite telemetry:

  • GPS data is strictly utilized for geofence enforcement (preventing unauthorized cross-border exit outside the United Arab Emirates) and urgent roadside rescue.
  • Real-time telemetry feeds are encrypted in transit and in storage.
  • 30-Day Retention Schedule: Historical route telemetry is automatically purged and permanently overwritten thirty (30) calendar days after the vehicle is returned and cleared of RTA traffic penalties.

5. Your Rights Under UAE Data Protection Law (PDPL)

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021, you maintain comprehensive data subject rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete audit extract of all personal data held regarding your profile.
  • Right to Rectification: Request prompt updating of any inaccurate identification details.
  • Right to Erasure (Oblivion): Request total deletion of your profile and records following statutory tax and RTA retention requirements.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit the use of your data exclusively to regulatory compliance.
