2. Vehicle Acquisition & Outright Purchase Terms

For outright acquisitions of new, bespoke, or certified pre-owned (CPO) motor vehicles from our Dubai showroom inventory:

  • Reservation Deposit: A minimum security deposit of 10% of the vehicle's total agreed AED price is required to place an allocation hold on any vehicle. Holds remain effective for fourteen (14) calendar days unless mutually extended in writing.
  • Vehicle Inspection Certificate: All certified pre-owned vehicles undergo our proprietary 120-point mechanical, structural, electronic, and aesthetic inspection, accompanied by official RTA Technical Inspection and GCC-spec validation papers.
  • Payment Settlement: Vehicle handover occurs strictly upon 100% receipt and cleared funds in LuxeDrive's UAE corporate bank account via wire transfer, verified banker's draft, Central Bank escrow, or compliant digital asset settlement.
  • Title & RTA Registration: LuxeDrive’s VIP concierge expedites complete title transfer, RTA luxury plate registration, and export documentation directly on behalf of the Client.

3. Luxury & Exotic Rental Eligibility and Tariffs

Clients renting high-performance supercars, grand tourers, or luxury saloons must fulfill the following mandatory criteria prior to vehicle dispatch:

  • Minimum Age Requirement: The primary renter must be at least twenty-one (21) years of age for luxury sedans and SUVs, and at least twenty-five (25) years of age for hypercars and track-focused models (e.g., Ferrari 296 GTB, Porsche 911 GT3 RS, Lamborghini Revuelto).
  • Valid Driver's License: UAE residents must hold a valid UAE driving license (minimum 6 months validity). International visitors must possess a valid international driving permit (IDP) alongside their original national driving license and passport with UAE entry stamp.
  • Security Deposit Pre-Authorization: A refundable security pre-authorization (AED 3,000 to AED 15,000 depending on vehicle tier) will be held via major credit card to cover prospective RTA Salik toll charges, municipal traffic fines, or fuel discrepancies. Any unused balance is released strictly within 14–21 business days following official RTA traffic fine clearance.
  • Mileage Allowances & Overage: Standard rental contracts include 250 km per day. Excess mileage will be billed at the specific model rate published on the rental contract (AED 5 to AED 25 per additional kilometer).

4. Prohibited Uses & Vehicle Care

The Client agrees that the vehicle shall be operated with utmost responsibility. The following activities constitute an absolute breach of contract and will trigger immediate vehicle deactivation and forfeiture of all deposits:

  • Operation on race tracks, drag strips, drifting events, or speed trials (unless under an official LuxeDrive track day package supervised by licensed personnel).
  • Off-road operation, desert dune driving, or driving on unpaved surfaces for non-desert rated supercars.
  • Sub-leasing, lending, or permitting unauthorized drivers not named in the signed rental agreement to operate the vehicle.
  • Smoking, vaping, or burning of incense/oud inside the vehicle. A deep sterilization and ozone cleaning fee of AED 2,500 will apply if violated.
  • Operating the vehicle under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or prescription medication impairing motor function.
